Vocabulary Lab

Learn vocabulary as connected knowledge—not as isolated translations.

Do not learn lonely words

When you learn only one translation, you may recognize a word but still be unable to use it. Build a lexical entry with the word’s form, meaning, grammar, and common partners.

Example: learn insist on doing something, not only insist = 主張する.
