Memory methods

Learning Strategies

Use retrieval, feedback, spacing, and purposeful reuse to remember vocabulary for longer.

🔍

Notice

Find the word in a useful sentence. Mark stress, word class, and a phrase partner.

🧠

Retrieve

Close the book. Recall the meaning, then recall the English form without choices.

📅

Space

Review after increasing intervals instead of repeating everything in one sitting.

Active recall

Testing is also learning

Rereading can feel easy because the answer is visible. Retrieval practice is harder: you produce the meaning or word from memory. The difficulty is useful when feedback follows.

Upgrade a flashcard:
Front: “express ______ (反対・不賛成を示す)”
Back: “express disapproval” + one example sentence
