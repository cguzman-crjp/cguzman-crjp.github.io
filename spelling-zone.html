Spelling strategy

Look–say–cover–write–check

  1. Look carefully at the whole word.
  2. Say it and mark the stressed syllable.
  3. Cover it.
  4. Write it from memory.
  5. Check, circle the difficult part, and retry.

間違えた部分だけを色で囲み、数分後と翌日にもう一度書いてみましょう。
