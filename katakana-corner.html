Katakana Corner

Turn loanwords into a learning advantage while avoiding pronunciation and meaning traps.

Katakana can help—and mislead

Many English words are familiar through Japanese loanwords. This can speed up meaning recognition, but the English pronunciation, stress, spelling, or meaning may be different.

カタカナ語は「ヒント」として使い、英語の発音・スペル・意味を必ず確認しましょう。
