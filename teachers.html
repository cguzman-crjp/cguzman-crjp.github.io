Teacher Guide

Use the website as a structured vocabulary-learning sequence and collect evidence of deeper lexical knowledge.

Purpose and audience

This prototype targets Japanese secondary and first-year university ESL learners around CEFR A2–B1. It can be used for self-study, station work, homework, or blended lessons.

Suggested implementation

  1. Diagnose prior knowledge.
  2. Select 6–10 productive targets.
  3. Teach form, meaning, and one phrase frame.
  4. Use a closed-book retrieval task.
  5. Require selected words in speaking or writing.
  6. Recycle after one day, one week, and three weeks.

Assessment evidence

  • recognition: choose meaning in context
  • meaning recall: explain a visible English word
  • form recall: produce English from a cue
  • orthography: write from sound or memory
  • depth: complete collocation or word family
  • productive use: use assigned words appropriately
